About me

The artist behind CGHEVEN.

Ammar Khan

I'm a VFX artist who specialises in volumetric simulations, and the founder of CGHEVEN and HevenPlay. I make every effect on CGHEVEN myself, in-house: nothing scraped, nothing resold.

CGHEVEN was created to give the global CG community high-quality, affordable CG assets: VDBs, VFX elements, 3D models, HDRIs and textures. My mission is to make professional-grade tools accessible to every creator, regardless of budget.

Alongside the assets, I teach how to use them in free tutorials and courses, build free add-ons for creative apps, and run HevenPlay, the games side of CGHEVEN.

Why I started CGHEVEN

A student should have the same tools as a studio in Hollywood. But good VFX assets were locked behind expensive packs, and most artists I knew, especially students, could not afford them.

So I started simulating effects myself and sharing them for free. People around the world began using them in their edits, ads and films, and that is how CGHEVEN grew.

Today it is a library of real VFX with free 1K and 2K downloads, free add-ons for 8 apps and free tutorials. Pro keeps it running.

How I work

Every effect starts as a real simulation. For 3D scenes I simulate volumes in Houdini and deliver them as OpenVDB, so they drop straight into Blender, Houdini, Maya, Unreal Engine and renderers like Cycles, Octane, Redshift and Arnold.

For edits and films, the same effects come as ready-to-use video in MP4, ProRes 4444 and EXR, with green screen versions and 1K, 2K and 4K options.
