About me

Founder · VFX artist.

The artist behind CGHEVEN

Hi, I'm Ammar Khan.

I'm a VFX artist from Pakistan. I make every effect on CGHEVEN, and I teach how to use them, in English and Urdu.

CGHEVEN was created to give the global CG community high-quality, affordable CG assets: VDBs, VFX elements, 3D models, HDRIs and textures. The mission is to make professional-grade tools accessible to every creator, regardless of budget.

Why I started CGHEVEN

A student in Pakistan should have the same tools as a studio in Hollywood.

The problem

Good VFX assets were locked behind expensive packs. Most artists I knew, especially students, could not afford them.

So I made my own

I started simulating effects and sharing them for free. People around the world used them in their edits, ads and films.

CGHEVEN today

A library of real VFX with free 1K and 2K, free add-ons for 8 apps, and free lessons in English and Urdu. Pro keeps it running.

VFX & Simulation

Real VFX, ready to drop in: smoke, fire, explosions, steam and holograms in MP4, ProRes 4444 and EXR. I make every effect on CGHEVEN myself.

For 3D scenes, my VDB collections are simulated in Houdini and delivered as OpenVDB, so they work in Blender, Houdini, Maya, Unreal Engine and renderers like Cycles, Octane, Redshift and Arnold.

Founder · CGHEVEN

CGHEVEN

The platform I built for creators who can't afford expensive packs: a library of real VFX with free 1K and 2K, free add-ons for 8 apps, and free lessons in English and Urdu.

It has VFX elements, VDBs, 3D models, HDRIs and textures, all made in-house. The free library is CC0, so anyone can use it in any project with no credit needed.

Founder · HevenPlay

HevenPlay

HevenPlay is the mobile games division of CGHEVEN, founded by Ammar Khan: a VFX artist from Pakistan who makes every effect on CGHEVEN and teaches how to use them, in English and Urdu. It builds casual and relaxing Android games, made to be picked up and enjoyed at your own pace.

Each game is published on Google Play. No account is needed to play.
